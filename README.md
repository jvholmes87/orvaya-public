# Orvaya Studio

**Infinite Worlds. Untold Stories.**

Orvaya Studio is an independent, AI-assisted science-fiction anthology initiative exploring spacefaring societies, human and alien perspectives, and stories across multiple fictional universes.

## What we share
Our goal is to show the real creative and engineering work behind each production: concept development, systems engineering, selected requirements, production toolchains, human/AI contribution records, verification, and lessons learned.

## First project
**BOOTS — The Fall of Meridian** is an in-development cinematic short featuring Captain Jeremiah Holmes. Its placement in the Freeman's Reach timeline is pending continuity review.

## Transparency
Tools mentioned as candidates are not necessarily in use. Published evidence is reviewed for confidentiality, provenance, and licensing.
