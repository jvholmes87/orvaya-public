# BOOTS — The Fall of Meridian
**Status:** Concept and preproduction

A cinematic military science-fiction short following Captain Jeremiah Holmes during the defense of Meridian Colony. The story focuses on endurance, command responsibility and the human cost of conflict.

The project is being developed as a potential entry in the Freeman's Reach universe, pending continuity review.

No release date or completed film is announced. Full scripts and unreleased canon remain private.
